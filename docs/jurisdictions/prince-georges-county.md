# Prince George's County Site-Plan Baseline

Rule-pack code: `US-MD-PRINCE_GEORGES`  
Baseline version: `2026-07-21.1`

The package separately evaluates land disturbance, earth movement, one-acre construction stormwater, woodland conservation/existing TCP, environmental screening, driveway/right-of-way work, residential infill eligibility, survey authority, and professional release.

Key screening thresholds implemented are more than 5,000 square feet of disturbance, more than 100 cubic yards of earth movement, and one acre or more of total construction disturbance. These are distinct triggers; woodland clearing is not treated as general land disturbance.

Official authorities are Prince George's County DPIE, Prince George's Soil Conservation District, M-NCPPC Planning, and Maryland Department of the Environment. Each persisted rule version stores source URL/title, agency, effective date when available, last verification time, version, confidence, and human-review requirement.

Positive floodplain/wetland/stream GIS results require professional/agency determination and are not legal delineations. One-to-six-lot infill fronting an existing built street is only a candidate path until eligibility is verified. Existing TCP constraints remain relevant even when no new woodland clearing is proposed.

Before production rule activation, Operations must re-open every official source, capture the effective document/form revision, seed immutable rule versions, and run all scenario fixtures. Source-site availability alone is not proof that a threshold is current.
