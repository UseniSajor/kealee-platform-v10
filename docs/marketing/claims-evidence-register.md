# Public Claims Evidence Register

Marketing Operations owns this register. A claim marked `blocked` must not be
published until its evidence is attached and approved.

| Claim category | Required evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contracting legal entity | Formation record, approved legal name, address, and counsel-approved terms | Operating model confirmed by owner: Platform operated by Kealee Construction LLC; each project agreement names the contracting entity. Formation/address/counsel evidence must be attached before an absolute legal-entity claim is published. |
| Licensed architect/designer availability | Active license lookup, engagement agreement, covered jurisdictions, and assigned service owner | Workflow rule implemented: matched professional becomes professional of record only after written engagement. Per-professional evidence remains required before profile publication or regulated work. |
| Engineer or professional stamp | Active license lookup and an engagement workflow that assigns the qualified professional | Workflow rule implemented: agreement must identify professional, jurisdiction, license, insurance, scope, and sealed deliverable. Per-project evidence remains required. |
| Permit filing/expediting | Service scope, supported jurisdictions, responsible operator, and filing/refund policy | Public scope and refund limitations published. Professional-of-record and jurisdiction evidence remains per project. |
| Certified or lender-accepted estimate | Qualification of signer, certification standard, sample deliverable, and lender acceptance evidence | Blocked — operational evidence required |
| RSMeans/current cost data | Active subscription or license, dataset/version date, and documented production use | Blocked — evidence required |
| Escrow or protected payments | Executed provider agreement, supported transaction flow, disclosures, and reconciliation owner | Blocked — provider evidence required |
| Contractor verification | Written verification standard and dated identity/license/insurance evidence per profile | Publication is blocked until production records contain current evidence. Once matched and engaged, the accepted agreement identifies the licensed and insured contractor of record. |
| Ratings, reviews, project counts | Source records and customer consent where applicable | Blocked — production source required |
| Delivery or response times | Measured service data and staffed SLA | Blocked — performance evidence required |
| Testimonials | Customer identity, completed engagement, quote approval, and publication consent | Blocked — evidence required |

The website may describe preliminary planning outputs and conditional optional
services without these claims, provided the limitation is visible before
purchase. Evidence should be reviewed at least quarterly and whenever a provider
or service workflow changes.
