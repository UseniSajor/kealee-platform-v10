Sec. 25-129. Credit and Security.
(a)
Existing trees to be preserved, proposed on-site woodland conservation, trees planted
throughout the site, and street trees located within the right-of-way along the property
frontage may be counted toward meeting this requirement. Calculations
of
tree canopy
