# Stormwater Calculations

Phase 1 implements versioned Rational Method screening, existing/proposed impervious comparison and preliminary BMP area sizing for micro-bioretention, dry wells, rain gardens, cisterns and permeable pavement.

Results include formula, inputs, units, rainfall source, rule version, intermediate area conversion, assumptions, warnings and professional-review status. BMP screening detects lack of space and always identifies infiltration, soil, overflow and underdrain/outfall review requirements. These calculations are preliminary and are not final hydrologic design.
