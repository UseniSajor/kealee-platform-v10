## 🏗️ GROWTH DRIVERS & LEVERS

### Project Owner Acquisition Channels

#### 1. **Organic/Word-of-Mouth** (Baseline 30%)
```
Timeline:        Immediate but slow
Cost:           Low ($0-50 per user)
Speed:          Slow (months to scale)
Quality:        High (repeat usage, referrals)
Scalability:    Limited to 30-40% of growth

Factors:
- User satisfaction (NPS > 50)
- Feature improvements
- Social sharing
- Content marketing
```

#### 2. **Paid Acquisition** (Growth 25-40%)
```
Timeline:        Immediate
Cost:           $100-300 per user
Speed:          Fast (weeks to see results)
Quality:        Medium (depends on targeting)
Scalability:    High (limited by budget)

Channels:
- Google Ads (Home renovation queries)
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- TikTok (Gen-Z homeowners)
- YouTube (Tutorial content)
- Pinterest (Design inspiration)

Best Performers:
- Google: 5-10% CAC efficiency
- Meta: 8-12% CAC efficiency
- TikTok: 15-20% CAC efficiency

Budget Allocation:
- Year 1: $2-5M
- Year 2: $10-20M
- Year 3: $20-50M
```

#### 3. **Strategic Partnerships** (Growth 20-30%)
```
Timeline:        3-6 months to negotiate, 6-12 months to implement
Cost:           Revenue share (10-30%) or upfront
Speed:          Medium (needs integration)
Quality:        Very high (trusted channels)
Scalability:    High (multiple partners)

Partners:
- Home Depot (50K+ stores, 100M annual visitors)
- Lowe's (2K+ stores, 80M annual visitors)
- Amazon (Home Services integration)
- Zillow (Home buying → renovation)
- Redfin (Post-purchase renos)
- Wayfair (Furniture → reno connection)
- Pinterest (Design discovery)

Expected Lift:
- Home Depot partnership: +500K-1M owners/year
- Amazon integration: +300K-500K/year
- Zillow integration: +200K-300K/year
```

#### 4. **Content Marketing** (Growth 10-15%)
```
Timeline:        3-6 months to build, ongoing
Cost:           Low ($500K-2M/year)
Speed:          Slow (6-12 months to ROI)
Quality:        Very high (engaged users)
Scalability:    High (compounding)

Content Types:
- Blog posts (renovation guides, cost guides)
- YouTube videos (transformation shows)
- TikTok content (quick reno tips)
- Instagram (design inspiration)
- Podcasts (homeowner stories)

Target:
- 100+ articles/month
- 10M+ annual page views
- 50+ YouTube videos
- 200+ TikTok videos
```

#### 5. **Referral Programs** (Growth 5-10%)
```
Timeline:        Immediate
Cost:           $50-100 per referral (credits/cash)
Speed:          Fast (viral potential)
Quality:        High (self-selected quality)
Scalability:    Medium (depends on incentives)

Program Design:
- $100 credit for referring a friend
- Friend gets $50 discount
- Contractor gets access discount
- Compound: refer 3+ = increased rewards

Viral Coefficient: 1.2-1.5x
- Each user refers 1-2 others
```

#### 1. **Direct Sales/Partnerships** (Primary 40-50%)
```
Timeline:        1-6 months per partnership
Cost:           $1,000-10,000 per contractor onboarded
Speed:          Medium
Quality:        Very high (vetted, committed)
Scalability:    Limited (requires manual work)

Strategy:

- Pitch: Access to 100K+ homeowners, lead gen, branding
- Onboarding: 1-week support for setup

Year 1 Target: 1,000+ contractors
Year 2 Target: 5,000+ contractors
Year 3 Target: 20,000+ contractors
```

```
Timeline:        Continuous
Cost:           $100-500 per signup (support)
Speed:          Very fast (automated)
Quality:        Medium (some churn)
Scalability:    Very high

Requirements:
- Simple signup (email, business info)
- License verification (automated)
- Payment setup (Stripe)
- Profile creation (30 min)
- First project in 24 hours

Conversion:
- Signup: 20% of invites
- Active use: 40% of signups
- Net 8% of invites → active
```

#### 3. **Organic/Referrals** (15-25%)
```
Timeline:        Ongoing, accelerates over time
Cost:           $200-500 per contractor
Speed:          Medium (compounds over time)
Quality:        Very high (peer-referred)
Scalability:    Very high

Drivers:
- Existing contractors referring peers
- Success stories (case studies)

- Awards/recognition

Growth Rate: 2-3x year 1, 1.5x year 2+
(Network effects of contractors wanting to be where work is)
```

#### 4. **Partnerships/Marketplaces** (10-20%)

Quality:        High
