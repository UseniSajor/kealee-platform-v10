# Yocum Properties — what the approved plans actually show

Written description compiled for text-to-video prompting. Two sources, and they
behave differently:

- **Technical set** (20 pp, 36x24) — text extractable. Plant schedule, lighting,
  notes.
- **Footprint & Setbacks sheet** (2 pp, 30x42, 1"=40') — **2 characters of
  extractable text.** It is vector linework with lettering drawn as paths, so
  everything below from that sheet was READ OFF THE RENDERED IMAGE, not parsed.

## Project identity

Election District, Prince George's County, Maryland. Design Engineering

WSSC 216SE03, Liber 39771 Folio 333.

## Layout

A 19-lot single-family subdivision on rolling wooded ground. **Joseph Drive**
runs through the centre as a curved residential street and terminates in a
cul-de-sac; **Glynis Road** and **Norris Way** bound the east side, both at 50 ft
right-of-way. The plan carries a match line to sheet 4, so the community
continues north.

Lots are LARGE and irregular, not a uniform grid — read from the sheet:
Lot 5 at 30,280 SF, Lot 8 at 30,906 SF, Lot 9 at 71,399 SF, Lot 10 at 52,087 SF,
Lot 11 at 52,528 SF, Lot 13 at 30,567 SF, Lot 14 at 30,331 SF, and one lot at
41,040 SF. That is roughly **0.7 to 1.6 acres per lot** — an estate-scale
subdivision, not a tract product. Homes sit well apart with mature trees between
them. Adjoining parcels 238 and 108 are labelled residential and vacant.

## Streets and surfaces

Concrete curb and gutter. **Concrete walk, 5 ft wide.** Concrete pavement
driveway aprons at the street. Bituminous concrete pavement for the roadway and
drives, with some existing bituminous to be milled 2" and repaved. Sod and seed
for disturbed areas.

## Lighting

PEPCO maintains the street lights. Fixtures are CLA-1-T3-32L-7-30K on 8 ft
poles — low, warm 3000K residential lighting, not highway cobra heads.

## Planting

Willow Oak (Quercus phellos), 29 specified at 2" calibre. Red maple. Norway
spruce as evergreen screen. Plan note: "large growing plants are not to be
planted in front of windows or under building overhangs."

## Ground and terrain

Rolling. Soils are Croom-Marr Urban Land Complex 5–15%, Croom-Howell-Collington
25–40%, Beltsville-Urban Land 5–15%, Croom gravelly sandy loam 5–10%, and
Widewater-Issue soils, poorly drained, in the low ground. The 25–40% slope
classes mean **real topographic relief** — this is not a flat site.

## NOT in the plans

No elevations, no floor plans, no façade materials, no roof type, no window or
door schedule, no interior finishes, no bed/bath counts, no pricing. The set is
civil only. Any building appearance in a render is invented.
