## What was asked and what was done
- **Mixed use, all buildings** — 7 mixed-use parcels (7 buildings) shoulder to shoulder along the full Gunpowder Drive frontage, downtown block form: 100 × 70 ft footprint each on the street line, retail at grade (49,000 sf), four residential floors, 22 dwellings each (154 du), 5 storeys ≈ 62 ft. Height cap in the certified CGO table is **86 ft** for multifamily (Sec. 27-4203) — the 88 ft mentioned is not what the table says; 62 ft is used and there is room to go taller.
- **Townhomes / two-over-twos where they fit** — 14 two-over-two dwellings (7 bays) and 3 townhouses on the two private streets behind the frontage buildings.
- **Access from Gunpowder Drive only** — every internal street tees off Gunpowder (existing 80' public R/W); no new access to Indian Head Highway (MD 210) or Livingston Road. Buildings front Gunpowder at street level.
- **North side reserved for stormwater** — 107,627 sf (2.47 ac) north of N 386,145 kept for the approved SDP's stormwater facility and its expansion; nothing is built there.
- **Additional SWM per requirements** — ESD (MDE Ch. 5): impervious 150,420 sf (32.7%), Rv 0.3444, ESDv 13,197 cf at P_E 1.0 in → 16,496 sf of micro-bioretention. The 107,627 sf reserved (existing facility and its expansion) exceeds twice the 16,496 sf of practice the drawn programme needs at P_E 1.0 in; the balance is forebay, access, freeboard and the higher P_E the soils may require. Micro-bioretention in every surface parking lot island and a bioswale along each private street, credited under ESD; rooftop disconnection is not available over parking. Design per the DPIE stormwater concept approval that precedes the DSP.
