## What was asked and what was done
- **Mixed use, all buildings** — 7 mixed-use parcels: 5 shoulder to shoulder on the Gunpowder Drive street line and 2 on the first internal street, downtown block form: 100 × 70 ft building at the street line, retail at grade (49,000 sf), four residential floors, 22 dwellings each (154 du), 5 storeys ≈ 62 ft against the certified 86-ft cap (the 88 ft mentioned is not what the table says). Surface parking behind each building on its own parcel.
- **Townhomes / two-over-twos where they fit** — 8 two-over-two dwellings (4 bays) and 1 townhouse on the internal streets; the connector street behind the frontage row took most of the interior rows.
- **Access from Gunpowder Drive only** — two internal streets tee off Gunpowder (existing 80' public R/W) and a connector behind the frontage row joins them into a loop, so no internal street dead-ends; no new access to Indian Head Highway (MD 210) or Livingston Road. Buildings front their street at street level.
- **North side reserved for stormwater** — 107,627 sf (2.47 ac) north of N 386,145 kept for the approved SDP's stormwater facility and its expansion; nothing is built there.
- **Additional SWM per requirements** — ESD (MDE Ch. 5): impervious 150,420 sf (32.7%), Rv 0.3444, ESDv 13,197 cf at P_E 1.0 in → 16,496 sf of micro-bioretention. The 107,627 sf reserved (existing facility and its expansion) exceeds twice the 16,496 sf of practice the drawn programme needs at P_E 1.0 in; the balance is forebay, access, freeboard and the higher P_E the soils may require. Micro-bioretention in every surface parking lot island and a bioswale along each private street, credited under ESD; rooftop disconnection is not available over parking. Design per the DPIE stormwater concept approval that precedes the DSP.

## Totals
**163 dwelling units + 49,000 sf retail** on the 10.556-ac tract (15.44 du/ac gross). Every type is within its certified density and coverage cap. **Parking is the constraint:** 243 surface spaces are drawn plus 10 in garages = 253, against 441 at the assumed ratios (1.5/du + 4 per 1,000 sf retail; Sec. 27-6300 is not in the engine's certified tables). Closing that gap means a parking deck under or behind the frontage buildings, a shared-parking reduction, or fewer dwellings.
